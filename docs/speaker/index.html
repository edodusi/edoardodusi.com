02Talks

A selection. Each one can be tailored to your audience and time slot, and I'm happy to build something new for your event.

TalkEN / ITIntermediate

Total Recall: Why Your RAG Chatbot Makes Things Up

Tutorial-grade RAG chatbots confidently invent facts, and a bigger model rarely fixes it: smarter retrieval does. No RAG 101 here. Instead, four upgrades with before-and-after metrics: structure-aware chunking, hybrid dense and BM25 search, two-stage retrieval with cross-encoder reranking, and evaluations like Recall@k that tell you whether any of it worked.

Given at Codemotion Roma and RomaJS, 2026

Talk35 min + Q&AEN

Honey, I Shrunk the Bits: How Modern HTTP Compression Works

From lossless compression basics to Gzip, Brotli and Zstandard: how each algorithm works, how they compare on real payloads, and how to configure them in a modern web stack. For JavaScript, performance and DevOps engineers, with config templates and a benchmark suite to take home.

Watch at Øredev 2025

Talk40 min + Q&AEN / IT

Bundlers: A Deep Dive Into Modern JavaScript Build Tools

What actually happens when you run a build? A tour of bundler internals: ASTs, dependency graphs, tree-shaking, code splitting and hot module replacement, and how Vite, webpack and Rollup each approach them. No live coding; a companion GitHub repository lets attendees dig in afterwards.

Watch at JSNation 2025 · Øredev 2025

Keynote45 min + Q&AEN

Open Source: From Revolution to Evolution

From the free software versus open source split to the cloud-era licensing wars: how open source won, what it cost, and what the next decade might look like. A keynote that ends on an uncomfortable question: what comes after open source?

Watch the phpday 2025 opening keynote

TalkEN / IT

Server Components Wars: PHP Strikes Back

A Star Wars-flavoured face-off between React Server Components and classic server-side PHP: rendering, data fetching, hydration, SSR and bundling. Which side really owns the server, and what can each learn from the other?

Watch at React Summit 2025

KeynoteEN / IT

True Wizards: The Story of John von Neumann and Alan Turing

Two lives that shaped modern computing, and the ideas they left us: the stored-program architecture, codebreaking and the first questions about machine intelligence. A storytelling keynote for any developer audience.

Watch at DevFest 2023

Short bio

Edoardo Dusi is a Senior Developer Advocate at AWS, part of the EMEA Developer Relations team. An engineer turned advocate, he spent years building web platforms and leading teams before moving into developer relations, most recently leading the DevRel & Community team at Storyblok. Based in Bologna, Italy, he speaks at conferences across Europe and hosts the Italian tech podcast Buongiorno da Edo.

Long bio

Edoardo Dusi is a Senior Developer Advocate at Amazon Web Services, part of the EMEA Developer Relations team, where he helps developers build on AWS through talks, workshops and online content.

He started out as a software engineer and spent close to a decade at SparkFabrik, as a backend engineer, frontend engineer and lead developer, before becoming the company's first Developer Relations Engineer. He then led the Developer Relations & Community team at Storyblok, working on SDKs, developer content and community, before joining AWS in 2026.

Edoardo has spoken at React Summit, JSNation, Øredev, WeAreDevelopers World Congress, SymfonyCon, phpday and reactjsday, among many others, and was a Sessionize Most Active Speaker in 2023, 2024 and 2025. His writing has appeared in Smashing Magazine, The New Stack and on the OpenSSF blog.

He writes and hosts Buongiorno da Edo, an Italian podcast about tech, AI and the internet with more than 300 episodes, and previously created the Continuous Delivery podcast. Off stage, he has a soft spot for the history of computing and video games.
